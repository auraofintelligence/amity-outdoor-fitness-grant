# Consultation plan

## Purpose

Gather local support, objections and practical fixes before asking for major money.

## Ask while people can see the idea

- Which gear would you use?
- How dense should the equipment be across the 100m+ strip?
- Where do people cross or park now?
- Have you seen koala tours, wildlife watchers or visitors step near the road while looking up?
- Would a crossing and calmer traffic help?
- What should stay open for bus, service or emergency access?
- Should water, shade and seating outrank extra equipment?
- What would need to change before you fully support it?

## Who to ask

- older locals
- families
- teens
- nearby residents
- campers and visitors
- skaters and reserve users
- koala tour operators or guides
- Council officers
- local tradies and suppliers
- Quandamooka representatives through appropriate channels

## Evidence to collect

- short notes
- support letters
- simple photos
- current quotes
- Council advice
- changes made because of feedback
