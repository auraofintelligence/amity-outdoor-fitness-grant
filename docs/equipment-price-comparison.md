## Budget reading

- A small static cluster may sit around the lower grant range, before access works.
- A useful mixed park needs equipment, surfacing, freight, installation, shade, seating and signs.
- Crossing, speed calming, potholes, drainage and water should be scoped separately with Council.

## Quote request

Ask for one-page itemised quotes covering supply, freight, installation, surfacing, coastal warranty, maintenance, spare parts and public-space compliance.
