# Grant options — plain and simple

Current grant information changes quickly. Treat this as a shortlist and verify every round before applying.

## Best local pathway

### 1. Redland Rapid Response Grant

**Best use:** small seed funding, consultation, launch event, small portable equipment, QR signs, community survey.  
**Why it helps:** quick, simple and local.  
**Scale:** $500–$3,000 when open.

### 2. Mayor and Councillors Community Benefit Fund

**Best use:** small local support, signs, community event, pilot activity, minor equipment.  
**Why it helps:** good for a small visible first step.  
**Scale:** up to $3,000 for organisations.

### 3. Redland Community Support Grant

**Best use:** small project works, local wellbeing, recreation and social connection.  
**Why it helps:** the project has clear local community benefit.  
**Scale:** up to $20,000.

### 4. Redland Build and Thrive Grant

**Best use:** main local anchor grant for the happy medium build.  
**Why it helps:** supports community infrastructure, accessibility, inclusion and resilience.  
**Scale:** up to $85,500 over three years.

## Best Queensland pathway

### 5. Queensland Gambling Community Benefit Fund

**Best use:** equipment and facility improvements.  
**Why it helps:** broad community benefit grant that may support a chunk of the equipment or amenity cost.  
**Scale:** round cap must be checked when the next round is released.

### 6. Queensland Games On! Field of Play

**Best use:** serious medium or stretch infrastructure build.  
**Why it helps:** designed for sport and active recreation infrastructure.  
**Scale:** in the current guideline, urban/regional LGAs can apply for $50,000–$499,999; current ROI has closed, so this is a monitor/future-round pathway unless a partner already submitted.

### 7. Queensland Games On! Partnership Fund

**Best use:** only for a large active recreation precinct with major co-funding.  
**Why it helps:** relevant to bigger regional legacy projects.  
**Scale:** large projects starting around $500,000+; high co-contribution expected.

## Best federal pathway

### 8. Stronger Communities Programme

**Best use:** small federal contribution to community infrastructure.  
**Why it helps:** can fill a $10,000–$20,000 gap in a funding stack.  
**Scale:** up to $20,000, generally through local MP invitation.

### 9. Play Our Way

**Best use:** future opportunity if the project has a strong women and girls participation focus.  
**Why it helps:** the programme targets access to sport and physical activity for women and girls.  
**Status:** closed at the time of this draft; monitor future rounds.

### 10. Growing Regions / federal infrastructure programmes

**Best use:** only for a much larger precinct-scale project.  
**Why it helps:** large capital infrastructure programmes can fund community amenity and liveability projects.  
**Status:** current rounds noted in this research are closed; monitor GrantConnect.

## Plain assessor logic

The strongest simple grant argument is:

1. The place is public and already used for recreation.
2. The project removes cost and access barriers.
3. The design supports strength, mobility, mental wellbeing and social connection.
4. The materials are suitable for a salty coastal site.
5. The build is staged, measurable and maintainable.
6. The project has local consultation, permission and co-funding.
