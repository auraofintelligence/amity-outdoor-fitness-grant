# Plain-English one-pager

## Project

**Amity Point Outdoor Fitness & Wellbeing Circuit**

## What we are asking for

Funding to build a free outdoor fitness and wellbeing circuit at Amity Point Recreation Reserve, Claytons Road, Amity.

## What the project will do

The project will install strong, salt-resistant public exercise equipment and simple wellbeing features so people can move, stretch, strengthen and connect outdoors.

It will include:

- bodyweight exercise equipment
- gentle mobility and balance stations
- a stretch, yoga and Tai chi area
- shade, seating and clear signs
- QR workout routines
- accessible paths and safe surfaces
- a launch programme and simple usage tracking

## Why it is needed

People are more likely to be active when the place is free, close, visible and easy to use. Amity has a strong outdoor lifestyle, but a dedicated public fitness and wellbeing circuit would make healthy movement easier for residents, older people, young people, families and visitors.

## Who will benefit

- local residents
- older adults
- young people
- women and girls
- people without gym access
- visitors and campers
- walking, skating and community groups

## What makes it suitable for Amity

The equipment will be selected for a salty coastal environment. The design will favour low-maintenance, fixed equipment with strong coatings, marine-grade fixings, safe surfaces and simple maintenance checks.

## What we will measure

- visits per week
- launch event attendance
- QR workout opens
- local feedback
- maintenance checks
- number of accessible and beginner-friendly exercise options

## Simple pitch

This project will create a free, salt-resistant outdoor fitness and wellbeing circuit at Amity Point so residents and visitors can strengthen, stretch, move safely, meet others and enjoy healthy activity close to home.
