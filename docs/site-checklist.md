# Site checklist — before a grant is submitted

Use this before asking for quotes or lodging a grant.

## 1. Confirm site details

- exact project footprint
- land owner / land manager
- lot and plan number
- current reserve purpose
- permitted uses
- whether the proposal is inside Amity Point Recreation Reserve or adjoining land
- whether any part is road reserve, coastal reserve or protected vegetation area

## 2. Get written permission early

Ask Redland City Council for advice on:

- landowner consent
- planning or operational works approvals
- building approval for shade structures, if needed
- public liability requirements
- maintenance responsibility
- procurement requirements
- whether Council must own the final asset
- whether an incorporated association or auspice body can apply

## 3. Check cultural and native title matters

Minjerribah is Quandamooka Country. Before using cultural material, stories, artwork, names or interpretive signs:

- seek Council advice
- engage appropriately with Quandamooka representatives
- budget for paid cultural consultation where relevant
- do not use cultural symbols or stories without permission
- keep early grant wording respectful and simple

Suggested early wording:

> The project will seek appropriate advice and engagement before any cultural, interpretive or public art elements are designed or installed.

## 4. Check environmental constraints

- tree roots and canopy
- coastal wind and salt exposure
- sand movement
- drainage after rain
- erosion risk
- protected vegetation
- wildlife and habitat
- heat and shade
- sightlines for passive surveillance

## 5. Check underground and site services

Before design is final:

- Dial Before You Dig / Before You Dig Australia search
- water lines
- sewer lines
- electrical lines
- irrigation
- stormwater
- telecommunications
- service access for construction vehicles

## 6. Check access and inclusion

- firm path from existing parking / road / paths
- wheelchair turning spaces
- rest seating
- shade near rest points
- options for older adults and beginners
- clear beginner instructions
- lighting needs and night-use policy
- visibility from road or active areas

## 7. Safety and standards

Ask suppliers and installers to confirm:

- public outdoor fitness equipment standards
- compliant surfacing where required
- fall zones and clear zones
- trip hazard controls
- anti-entrapment design
- pinch point controls
- inspection schedule
- maintenance schedule
- warranty
- spare parts
- vandal-resistant fixings

## 8. Coastal materials checklist

Ask suppliers to specify:

- hot-dip galvanised steel after fabrication, marine-grade stainless steel, or marine-grade aluminium
- 316 stainless fasteners where suitable
- high-UV powder coating
- sealed bearings for any moving parts
- UV-stabilised plastics
- anti-graffiti or easy-clean coating where practical
- corrosion warranty for coastal conditions
- recommended wash-down and inspection schedule

## 9. Quote checklist

Every quote should show:

- equipment supply
- freight to Minjerribah
- installation
- site preparation
- surfacing
- shade
- seating
- signage
- water refill, if proposed
- tree or landscape works, if proposed
- design drawings
- certification
- contingencies
- GST
- ongoing maintenance estimate

## 10. Grant attachments to prepare

- one-page project summary
- site map or concept plan
- photos of the area
- letters of support
- landowner consent
- community consultation notes
- supplier quotes
- budget table
- maintenance plan
- risk plan
- evidence summary
- auspice or incorporation documents
- insurance certificate
