# Source notes

These notes summarise key sources used to prepare the draft website and grant material. Verify all grant dates and eligibility before applying.

## Site and local context

- Redland City Council lists **Amity Point Recreation Reserve, 24–42 Claytons Road, Amity** in its park/public open space material.
- Redland City Council lists Amity Point Recreation Reserve as an existing ramp park location on Claytons Road.
- Redland City Council states that temporary park activities such as fitness, yoga and Tai chi may require approval, and gives examples of health, wellbeing and social interaction activities.
- Redland City Council recognises the Quandamooka People as Traditional Custodians of much of Redlands Coast. Council material states that North Stradbroke Island / Minjerribah is central to Quandamooka native title and that Council works with Quandamooka representatives including QYAC.
- Queensland Government Minjerribah Futures states that the Quandamooka People are the traditional custodians of Minjerribah and that more than 90% of the island is under native title.

## Health and wellbeing evidence

- Queensland Chief Health Officer physical activity data: 2024 Queensland adult sufficient activity and inactivity estimates.
- AIHW physical activity data: adult physical activity, muscle-strengthening and age-group guideline gaps.
- Australian Government 24-hour movement guidelines: adults should do moderate-to-vigorous activity, muscle strengthening, functional balance / mobility and reduce sedentary time.
- Beyond Blue: physical activity can help mental wellbeing, mood, sleep, energy, worries and social connection.
- AIHW social isolation and loneliness data: 15% of Australians aged 15+ experiencing social isolation and 15% experiencing loneliness in 2023.
- Health and Wellbeing Queensland: green and blue spaces can support wellbeing, with examples including outdoor gym equipment, yoga and mindfulness.

## Outdoor fitness planning and safety

- Sport and Recreation Victoria outdoor fitness guidelines emphasise planning, design, activation, maintenance and evaluation.
- The same guidance supports universal design, CPTED, beginner/intermediate/advanced workout options, signage and maintenance checks.
- Kidsafe NSW outdoor fitness equipment guidance highlights surfacing, shade, seating, rain shelter, surveillance, toilets, bubblers, bins, lighting, signage, accessibility and quality equipment.

Useful URLs:

- https://sport.vic.gov.au/publications-and-resources/community-sport-resources/outdoor-fitness-equipment-guidelines

- https://www.productsafety.gov.au/products/health-lifestyle/sport-fitness-recreation/sport-exercise-play-equipment

## Notes for grant writing

Use the sources to support simple claims, not to overload the application. The strongest grant line is that the project removes practical barriers to healthy movement by providing free, safe, inclusive and durable public infrastructure close to where people already gather.
