Comparable projects

These examples help frame scale. They are not direct cost quotes for Amity.

Useful comparators

  • Brisbane has many outdoor gym locations, showing this is normal public recreation infrastructure.
  • a_space notes Brisbane City Council projects using static and dynamic outdoor fitness equipment with accessibility and signage.
  • Mapleton / Lilyponds community material shows grant funding used for outdoor fitness equipment while Council supported installation.
  • KOMPAN's HOTA Parklands case study shows a Gold Coast outdoor gym with calisthenics, resistance equipment, pathways and accessible equipment access.
  • Queensland Gambling Community Benefit Fund recipient lists include smaller outdoor fitness, shade and softfall grants.
  • Emu Park Lions reported a community outdoor fitness project costing more than $50,000 with grant assistance.

What to learn

  • Small equipment-only projects can be grant-sized.
  • Installation, surfacing and Council works can change the budget quickly.
  • Public projects are more persuasive when they include access, shade, maintenance and activation.
  • Amity should not copy another site. It should use comparators to ask for realistic quotes.
