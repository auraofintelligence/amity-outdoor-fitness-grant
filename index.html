Grant pathway

Use small grants for consultation and launch, medium grants for the core build, and larger grants only when designs, permissions and co-contributions are ready.

Delivery plan

A sensible path from idea to installation

1

Check the site

Confirm land tenure, tree roots, underground services, access, drainage, shade, visual safety and any Council or cultural heritage requirements.

2

Talk early

Speak with Council, local residents, island groups, youth, elders, mobility users, skaters, the community club and Quandamooka representatives.

3

Design simply

Prepare a one-page concept, a rough layout, equipment list, maintenance plan, quotes and a plain-English case for free public health infrastructure.

4

Fund in stages

Use small grants for design and activation, then larger grants for equipment, surfacing, shade, seating, signage and installation.

5

Measure outcomes

Track usage counts, QR scans, launch sessions, surveys, community feedback, maintenance checks and stories from local users.

This project will create a free, salt-resistant outdoor fitness and wellbeing circuit at Amity Point so residents and visitors can strengthen, stretch, move safely, meet others and enjoy healthy activity close to home.
